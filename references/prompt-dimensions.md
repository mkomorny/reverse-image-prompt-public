# Prompt Dimensions — Full Taxonomy

Use this reference to ensure no visual dimension is missed during image analysis.

---

## 1. Medium & Rendering Style

- **Medium**: photograph, digital painting, oil painting, watercolor, gouache, pencil sketch, charcoal, ink drawing, vector art, pixel art, 3D render, CGI, collage, mixed media, screen print, linocut, concept art, illustration
- **Rendering quality**: photorealistic, hyperrealistic, stylized, semi-realistic, cel-shaded, painterly, impressionistic, abstract, minimalist, maximalist
- **Finish**: matte, glossy, textured, clean, rough, polished
- **Era/genre**: retrofuturism, vaporwave, art nouveau, art deco, baroque, renaissance, ukiyo-e, brutalist, cyberpunk, cottagecore, dark fantasy, solarpunk, biopunk
- **Artist style references** (only when identifiable): e.g., "in the style of Zdzisław Beksiński", "reminiscent of James Gurney", "Moebius linework"

---

## 2. Subject

### Primary Subject
- What is it (person, animal, object, landscape, abstract form)?
- Count (single, pair, group, crowd)
- Species / type / category

### Person / Character Details
- Age range (child, teen, young adult, middle-aged, elderly)
- Gender presentation
- Ethnicity / skin tone (describe objectively: e.g., "warm medium brown skin", "very fair complexion with visible freckles")
- Build: slim, athletic, stocky, heavyset
- Height impression: tall, petite, average
- Facial features: jawline shape, eye shape/color, nose shape, lip fullness, cheekbones, brow weight
- Hair: color, length, texture (wavy, straight, coily), style (loose, braided, updo, shaved), any highlights
- Makeup: none, natural, dramatic, specific colors
- Facial expression: neutral, smiling (teeth/no teeth), sad, fearful, determined, serene, etc.
- Head position: facing camera, 3/4 turn, profile, looking up/down
- Body pose: standing, seated, crouched, mid-action, reclining; arm/hand positions
- Clothing: every garment, color, material, pattern, fit (loose/tailored), condition (worn, pristine)
- Accessories: jewelry, glasses, hat, bag, shoes — describe each
- Distinguishing marks: tattoos, scars, birthmarks, facial hair

### Animal / Creature
- Species, breed if identifiable
- Markings, coloring patterns
- Pose, behavior (resting, alert, mid-motion)
- Emotional state / energy

### Object / Vehicle / Architecture
- Type, style, era
- Material, surface condition (rusted, polished, weathered, new)
- Scale relative to surroundings
- Distinguishing design features

---

## 3. Environment & Setting

- **Interior / exterior**
- **Location type**: forest, city street, bedroom, studio, desert, ocean, outer space, underwater, fantasy realm, etc.
- **Specific setting details**: architectural elements, foliage, floor/ground texture, ceiling, walls, horizon
- **Time of day**: dawn, midday, golden hour, blue hour, dusk, night, midnight
- **Season**: spring, summer, autumn, winter — conveyed by foliage, light quality, precipitation
- **Weather**: clear, overcast, foggy/misty, rainy, stormy, snowy, hazy
- **Background depth**: shallow (blurred background), mid-ground elements, deep environmental detail
- **Foreground elements**: anything between camera and subject

---

## 4. Composition & Framing

- **Shot type**: extreme close-up, close-up, medium shot, medium-long, long shot, extreme long shot, aerial/bird's-eye, worm's-eye
- **Framing**: centered, rule of thirds, leading lines, frame-within-frame, symmetrical, asymmetrical
- **Angle**: straight-on (0°), slight low angle, strong low angle, slight high angle, overhead (top-down), dutch tilt (angle in degrees if discernible)
- **Depth of field**: shallow (subject sharp, background bokeh), deep (everything sharp), selective focus
- **Bokeh quality**: circular, hexagonal, creamy, harsh
- **Negative space**: present, used deliberately, absent
- **Aspect ratio**: square (1:1), portrait (2:3, 4:5, 9:16), landscape (3:2, 16:9, 21:9), panoramic
- **Lens feel**: wide-angle distortion, telephoto compression, fisheye, tilt-shift miniaturization
- **Visual weight distribution**: where does the eye go first, second, third?

---

## 5. Lighting

- **Primary source type**: natural sunlight, overcast diffused, golden-hour sun, blue-hour ambient, moonlight, studio key light, neon/practical lights, fire, candlelight, bioluminescence, screen glow, mixed
- **Direction**: front-lit, side-lit (left/right), backlit/rim-lit, top-lit, under-lit, split lighting
- **Quality**: hard (sharp shadows), soft (diffused, gradual transitions), dappled, volumetric (god rays, light shafts)
- **Color temperature**: warm (2700–3500K feel), neutral (5000–6000K), cool (7000K+), mixed warm/cool
- **Shadow character**: deep black shadows, soft penumbra, colored shadows, no shadows
- **Highlights**: specular hot spots, blown-out, controlled, gradient
- **Rim light / separation light**: present/absent, color
- **Ambient fill**: strong fill (low contrast), weak fill (high contrast), colored ambient
- **Lens flare / light bleed**: present, style (anamorphic streak, circular, sunburst)

---

## 6. Color & Tone

- **Dominant colors**: list top 2–4 colors by name and approximate hex if identifiable
- **Color harmony**: monochromatic, analogous, complementary, split-complementary, triadic, tetradic
- **Saturation level**: desaturated/muted, natural, vibrant, oversaturated, neon
- **Contrast**: low (flat, hazy), medium, high (punchy), extreme (pure blacks and whites)
- **Color grading / look**: cinematic teal-orange, faded film, high-key clean, dark and gritty, vintage warm, pastel dreamy, neon cyberpunk
- **Skin tone rendering** (if people present): neutral, warm-shifted, cool-shifted, stylized
- **Black point**: crushed blacks, lifted/faded blacks, true black
- **White point**: clean whites, warm whites, blown highlights

---

## 7. Texture & Surface Detail

- **Overall detail level**: low-detail/painterly, moderate, high-detail, hyper-detailed
- **Skin texture**: smooth, pored, weathered, young and smooth
- **Fabric/material rendering**: woven pattern, sheen, folds, wrinkles, transparency
- **Environmental textures**: bark, stone, concrete, water surface (calm/rippled/turbulent), metal, glass, foliage
- **Noise/grain**: film grain (fine/coarse), digital noise, clean/noiseless
- **Paint strokes visible**: yes/no, direction, size
- **Linework**: present/absent, weight, style (contour, crosshatch, gestural)

---

## 8. Atmosphere & Mood

- **Emotional tone**: peaceful, tense, melancholic, joyful, mysterious, ominous, romantic, surreal, epic, intimate, playful, horror
- **Energy level**: calm/still, dynamic/kinetic, explosive
- **Narrative implication**: is something happening, about to happen, just happened?
- **Atmospheric effects**: fog, haze, smoke, dust particles, rain streaks, snow, falling leaves, floating embers, lens moisture/condensation
- **Scale impression**: intimate, human-scale, monumental/vast
- **Isolation vs. connection**: subject feels alone vs. connected to environment

---

## 9. Technical / Generator Tags

Include relevant quality and technical tags based on the image:

### Universal Quality Boosters
`masterpiece`, `best quality`, `ultra-detailed`, `sharp focus`, `professional`, `award-winning`

### Photography-Specific
`shot on [camera model if identifiable]`, `[focal length]mm lens`, `f/[aperture]`, `ISO [value]`, `RAW photo`, `DSLR`, `35mm film`

### 3D / CGI Specific
`octane render`, `unreal engine 5`, `ray tracing`, `global illumination`, `subsurface scattering`, `4K render`

### Aspect Ratio Guidance
- Square: `--ar 1:1` (MJ)
- Portrait: `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 9:16`
- Landscape: `--ar 16:9` or `--ar 3:2`
- Cinematic: `--ar 21:9`

### Negative Prompt Suggestions (Stable Diffusion / Flux)
Common negatives to add based on image type:
- Portrait: `ugly, deformed, extra limbs, bad anatomy, blurry, low quality, watermark, signature, duplicate`
- Landscape: `overexposed, washed out, jpeg artifacts, low resolution, blurry`
- Stylized art: `photorealistic, 3D render, stock photo, generic`

---

## 10. What Makes This Image Unique

This is the most important dimension. After cataloguing everything above, ask:

> "If I removed every detail that could describe a thousand other images, what single detail remains that is specific to THIS image?"

Capture that detail explicitly in the prompt. It might be:
- A specific gesture or micro-expression
- An unusual lighting setup not commonly generated
- A specific composition choice (e.g., subject positioned in extreme lower-left with vast negative space above)
- An unexpected color combination
- A texture or surface anomaly
- The relationship between two elements in the frame
